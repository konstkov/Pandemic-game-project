A group of healthcare professionals has created a bioweapon, and their goal is to spread it throughout Finland. The objective of the game is to infect all municipalities with airports by flying there and releasing the virus, all while managing limited crew members and points. To succeed, the player must hire new agents, which costs points. Everyone starts with 10 points. However, if the player runs out of points or time before infecting all the municipalities with airports in Finland, they fail the game and must start from the beginning. During the game, players can be rewarded with points based on their actions.
