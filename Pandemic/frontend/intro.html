Welcome to Pandemic

This immersive strategy game puts you in control of a team of healthcare professionals turned rogue, tasked with spreading a powerful bioweapon across Finland. Navigate a world of strategic challenges, limited resources, and random events as you attempt to infect all municipalities with airports before time or resources run out.

As the mastermind behind the operation, you will need to manage agents, adapt to unforeseen circumstances, and make critical decisions. Choose from multiple virus strains, each with unique strengths and weaknesses, to maximize your efforts while evading government interventions.

Playing the Game

Playing the game requires sharp strategic thinking:

  • Manage Your Resources: Start with 10 points and carefully allocate them to hire agents, unlock regions, and choose flight paths. Be mindful—running out of points means failure.
  • Choose Your Virus Strains: Pick from multiple strains with varying infectiousness and resistance. Decide which strain is best suited for each municipality to balance rapid spread and stealth.
  • Deploy Agents: Hire agents with special skills such as increased virus spread efficiency or immunity to the virus. Use them strategically to overcome challenges in high-difficulty regions.
  • Infect All Municipalities: Your ultimate goal is to infect every municipality with an airport in Finland before the time runs out or your points are depleted. Each turn matters, so plan wisely!

Key Features

  • Multiple Virus Strains: Select the best strain for each region, balancing rapid spread and stealth.
  • Event Cards/Random Events: Encounter events that alter gameplay dynamics, keeping the experience unpredictable.
  • Special Agent Classes: Deploy basic and elite agents, each with unique costs and capabilities.
  • Region-Specific Challenges: Face higher difficulty levels in some areas, requiring careful investment and strategy.
  • Time Pressure: Race against the clock to achieve your objective before it’s too late.

Prepare yourself for a thrilling journey of strategy, adaptation, and survival. Will you succeed in your mission, or will time and resource constraints bring your plans to a halt? The challenge awaits!
